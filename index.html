Check a sealed message

Long-press the message in WhatsApp → Copy, then paste the whole message here.

What just happened?

  1. The sender seals a message once, with a key only they hold.
  2. It can be forwarded, copied or re-shared any number of times.
  3. Anyone can check who sent it and that not a word has changed.
