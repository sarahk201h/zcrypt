Check before you act.

Notices and forwarded messages can be faked, and the fake looks exactly like the real one. Zcrypt lets your phone check whether what you received is genuine and unchanged.

Why this matters

In a crisis, people act on information that reaches them second-hand: an evacuation route, a distribution time, a school notice. One changed word can send a family to the wrong place.

Zcrypt attaches proof to the words themselves, so it survives forwarding, copying and printing, in English and Arabic. When something has been changed, it shows what the real notice said.
